amlānahṛṣyadavannītalakīrṇapuṣpau
śrīveṅkaṭādriśikhirābharaṇāyamānau ।
ānanditākhilamanonayanau tavaitau
śrīveṅkaṭeśacaraṇau śaraṇaṃ prapadye ॥ 13 ॥
Translation
Feet on which unfading fresh flowers are ever scattered, which are the supreme ornament of Venkata's summit, which fill all minds and eyes with bliss — I take refuge at the feet of Venkatesha.
प्रायः प्रपन्नजनताप्रथमावगाह्यौ
मातुः स्तनाविव शिशोरमृतायमानौ ।
प्राप्तौ परस्परतुलामतुलान्तरौ ते
श्रीवेङ्कटेशचरणौ शरणं प्रपद्ये ॥ 14 ॥
IAST
prāyaḥ prapannajanatāprathamāvagāhyau
mātuḥ stanāviva śiśoramṛtāyamānau ।
prāptau parasparatulāmatulāntarau te
śrīveṅkaṭeśacaraṇau śaraṇaṃ prapadye ॥ 14 ॥
Translation
Drunk first with joy by the surrendered, like a baby at its mother's breast — matchless, yet each foot matching the other in equal splendour — I take refuge at the feet of Venkatesha.
सत्त्वोत्तरैः सततसेव्यपदाम्बुजेन
संसारतारकदयार्द्रदृगञ्चलेन ।
सौम्योपयन्तृमुनिना मम दर्शितौ ते
श्रीवेङ्कटेशचरणौ शरणं प्रपद्ये ॥ 15 ॥ ( 2 )
IAST
sattvottaraiḥ satatasevyapadāmbujena
saṃsāratārakadayārdradṛgañcalena ।
saumyopayantṛmuninā mama darśitau te
śrīveṅkaṭeśacaraṇau śaraṇaṃ prapadye ॥ 15 ॥ ( 2 )
Translation
These feet were shown to me by Saumyopayantri Muni (Tiruvaymozhi Pillai) — one whose pure mind is ever devoted to those lotus feet, whose compassionate gaze rescues souls from samsara — I take refuge at the feet of Venkatesha.
श्रीश श्रिया घटिकया त्वदुपायभावे
प्राप्ये त्वयि स्वयमुपायतया स्फुरन्त्या ।
नित्याश्रिताय निरवद्यगुणाय तुभ्यं
स्यां किङ्करो वृषगिरीश न जातु मह्यम् ॥ 16 ॥ ( 2 )
इति श्रीवेङ्कटेशप्रपत्तिः ॥
IAST
śrīśa śriyā ghaṭikayā tvadupāyabhāve
prāpye tvayi svayamupāyatayā sphurantyā ।
nityāśritāya niravadyaguṇāya tubhyaṃ
syāṃ kiṅkaro vṛṣagirīśa na jātu mahyam ॥ 16 ॥ ( 2 )
>iti śrīveṅkaṭeśaprapattiḥ ॥
Translation
O Lord, with Shri (Lakshmi) as the means and you as the goal — you who are the eternal refuge of those who depend on you, of flawless attributes — may I become your servant, O lord of Vrishagiri, never belonging to myself alone.
Thus the Sri Venkatesha Prapatti is complete.

Maṅgalāśāsanam

श्रियः कान्ताय कल्याणनिधये निधयेऽर्थिनाम् ।
श्रीवेङ्कटनिवासाय श्रीनिवासाय मङ्गलम् ॥ 1 ॥ ( 2 )
IAST
śriyaḥ kāntāya kalyāṇanidhaye nidhaye'rthinām ।
śrīveṅkaṭanivāsāya śrīnivāsāya maṅgalam ॥ 1 ॥ ( 2 )
Translation
Auspiciousness (mangalam) to the beloved of Shri (Lakshmi), the ocean of auspicious qualities, the refuge of those in need — to Srinivasa, the dweller of Venkata hill, be all auspiciousness.
लक्ष्मीसविभ्रमालोकसुभ्रूविभ्रमचक्षुषे ।
चक्षुषे सर्वलोकानां वेङ्कटेशाय मङ्गलम् ॥ 2 ॥
IAST
lakṣmīsavibhramālōkasubhrūvibhramacakṣuṣē ।
cakṣuṣē sarvalōkānāṃ vēṅkaṭēśāya maṅgaḻam ॥ 2 ॥
Translation
Auspiciousness to Venkatesha, whose eyes are the very vision of all the worlds, captivating with the graceful glances of Lakshmi.
श्रीवेङ्कटाद्रिशृङ्गाग्रमङ्गलाभरणाङ्घ्रये ।
मङ्गलानां निवासाय वेङ्कटेशाय मङ्गलम् ॥ 3 ॥
IAST
śrīveṅkaṭādriśṛṅgāgramaṅgalābharaṇāṅghraye ।
maṅgalānāṃ nivāsāya vēṅkaṭēśāya maṅgalam ॥ 3 ॥
Translation
Auspiciousness to Srinivasa — whose lotus feet are the ornaments adorning the very summit of the Venkata hill, the abode of all auspiciousness.
सर्वावयवसौन्दर्यसम्पदा सर्वचेतसाम् ।
सदा सम्मोहनायास्तु वेङ्कटेशाय मङ्गलम् ॥ 4 ॥
IAST
sarvāvayavasaundaryasampadā sarvacetasām ।
sadā sammohanāyāstu veṅkaṭeśāya maṅgalam ॥ 4 ॥
Translation
Auspiciousness to Venkatesha, whose beauty of every limb eternally enchants the minds of all beings.
नित्याय निरवद्याय सत्यानन्दचिदात्मने ।
सर्वान्तरात्मने श्रीमद्वेङ्कटेशाय मङ्गलम् ॥ 5 ॥
IAST
nityāya niravadyāya satyānandacidātmane ।
sarvāntarātmane śrīmadveṅkaṭeśāya maṅgalam ॥ 5 ॥
Translation
Auspiciousness to Venkatesha — the eternal, the flawless, the embodiment of true bliss and pure consciousness, the inner self of all.
स्वतःसर्वविदे सर्वशक्तये सर्वशेषिणे ।
सुलभाय सुशीलाय वेङ्कटेशाय मङ्गलम् ॥ 6 ॥
IAST
svataḥsarvavide sarvaśaktaye sarvaśeṣiṇe ।
sulabhāya suśīlāya veṅkaṭeśāya maṅgalam ॥ 6 ॥
Translation
Auspiciousness to Venkatesha — who is all-knowing of himself, omnipotent, the supreme master of all, yet easily accessible and of gentle disposition.
परस्मै ब्रह्मणे पूर्णकामाय परमात्मने ।
प्रयुञ्जे परतत्त्वाय वेङ्कटेशाय मङ्गलम् ॥ 7 ॥
IAST
parasmai brahmaṇe pūrṇakāmāya paramātmane ।
prayuñje paratattvāya veṅkaṭeśāya maṅgalam ॥ 7 ॥
Translation
Auspiciousness to Venkatesha, the supreme Brahman, whose desires are ever fulfilled, the Paramatma, to whom I offer this prayer of auspiciousness.
आकारतत्त्वमश्रान्तमात्मनामनुपश्यताम् ।
अतृप्त्यमृतरूपाय वेङ्कटेशाय मङ्गलम् ॥ 8 ॥
IAST
ākāratattvamaśrāntamātmanāmanupaśyatām ।
atṛptyamṛtarūpāya veṅkaṭeśāya maṅgalam ॥ 8 ॥
Translation
Auspiciousness to Venkatesha — whose form brings insatiable delight even to those who behold him eternally, of the nature of immortal nectar.
प्रायः स्वचरणौ पुंसां शरण्यत्वेन पाणिना ।
कृपयाऽऽदिशते श्रीमद्वेङ्कटेशाय मङ्गलम् ॥ 9 ॥
IAST
prāyaḥ svacaraṇau puṃsāṃ śaraṇyatvena pāṇinā ।
kṛpayā''diśate śrīmadveṅkaṭeśāya maṅgalam ॥ 9 ॥
Translation
Auspiciousness to Venkatesha, who graciously extends his own feet to those who seek refuge, indicating them as the means of salvation.
दयामृततरङ्गिण्यास्तरङ्गैरिव शीतलैः ।
अपाङ्गैस्सिञ्चते विश्वं वेङ्कटेशाय मङ्गलम् ॥ 10 ॥
IAST
dayāmṛtataraṅgiṇyāstaraṅgairiva śītalaiḥ ।
apāṅgaissiñcate viśvaṃ veṅkaṭeśāya maṅgalam ॥ 10 ॥
Translation
Auspiciousness to Venkatesha — whose compassionate sidelong glances sprinkle the universe with cool waves of mercy like a river of ambrosia.
स्रग्भूषाम्बरहेतीनां सुषमाऽऽवहमूर्तये ।
सर्वार्तिशमनायास्तु वेङ्कटेशाय मङ्गलम् ॥ 11 ॥
IAST
sragbhūṣāmbarahetīnāṃ suṣamā''vahamūrtaye ।
sarvārtiśamanāyāstu veṅkaṭeśāya maṅgalam ॥ 11 ॥
Translation
Auspiciousness to Venkatesha, whose divine form bears garlands, ornaments, garments and weapons of dazzling splendour, the remover of all afflictions.
श्रीवैकुण्ठविरक्ताय स्वामिपुष्करिणीतटे ।
रमया रममाणाय वेङ्कटेशाय मङ्गलम् ॥ 12 ॥ ( 2 )
IAST
śrīvaikuṇṭhaviraktāya svāmipuṣkariṇītaṭe ।
ramayā ramamāṇāya veṅkaṭeśāya maṅgalam ॥ 12 ॥ ( 2 )
Translation
Auspiciousness to Venkatesha — who turned away from Vaikunta to sport with Ramaa on the banks of Svami Pushkarini on Venkatadri.
श्रीमत्सुन्दरजामातृमुनिमानसवासिने ।
सर्वलोकनिवासाय श्रीनिवासाय मङ्गलम् ॥ 13 ॥ ( 2 )
IAST
śrīmatsundarajāmātṛmunimānasavāsine ।
sarvalokanivāsāya śrīnivāsāya maṅgalam ॥ 13 ॥ ( 2 )
Translation
Auspiciousness to Srinivasa — who dwells in the heart of the great Acharya Sundara Jamamatri Muni (Manavala Mamunigal) and in the hearts of all worlds.
मङ्गलाशासनपरैर्मदाचार्यपुरोगमैः ।
सर्वैश्च पूर्वैराचार्यैः सत्कृतायास्तु मङ्गलम् ॥ 14 ॥
श्रीपद्मावतीसमेत श्रीश्रीनिवासपरब्रह्मणे नमः ॥
IAST
maṅgalāśāsanaparairmadācāryapurogamaiḥ ।
sarvaiśca pūrvairācāryaiḥ satkṛtāyāstu maṅgalam ॥ 14 ॥
śrīpadmāvatīsameta śrīśrīnivāsaparabrahmaṇe namaḥ ॥
Translation
May all auspiciousness be to the Lord, as proclaimed by the Acharyas — beginning with my own teacher — and all the previous Acharyas who offered this sacred mangalasasanam.
Salutations to Sri Srinivasa, the supreme Brahman, together with Sri Padmavati.
